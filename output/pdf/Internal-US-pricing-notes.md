Do not send these notes to clients. The client PDF omits costs and profit margins.

Target: at least 45% operating margin before income tax on Astra service revenue. USD rounding produces approximately 46.44% on development and 50.25% on care. This is not guaranteed after-tax net profit.

Preserved assumptions: INR400/hour labor; 12/24/42 build hours (including delivery, testing and expected warranty work); 0.5/1/2 hours of monthly care. Overhead: 15% of revenue for sales, administration, tools and rework. Added US collection-fee allowance: 3% of service revenue (a planning assumption, NOT a verified payment-provider tariff). Planning exchange rate: INR90/USD, retained from the Indian brochure; NOT a live rate. Verify actual exchange settlement, fixed/percentage payment fees, fee taxes and workload before each binding quote. Reprice if needed to preserve target.

Formula: required USD price = (INR labor cost / planning INR per USD) / (1 - 0.15 - 0.03 - 0.45). Round upward. Development floors: $144.15 / $288.29 / $504.51; quoted prices: $150 / $300 / $525. Care floors: $6.01 / $12.02 / $24.03; quoted $7 / $14 / $28. At 3% collection fees, build margin remains >=45% only if effective FX before that fee is at least INR86.49/USD, with labor/overhead unchanged. At INR90/USD, total collection fees can reach about 4.44% before build margin drops below45%. Fixed per-transaction charges on small monthly payments may exceed this reserve; use an appropriate low-fee method or re-quote before acceptance. Never automatically promise these fees with an unverified payment method.
